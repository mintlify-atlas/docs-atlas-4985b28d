## Welcome to Sn0w

Sn0w is a high-performance Minecraft 1.21.x hacked client developed over 5 years, widely considered one of the best clients for crystal PVP and anarchy servers like 2b2t. Built on a modified Kami5 base, Sn0w features numerous original exploits and bypasses developed specifically for competitive HVH (hacker vs hacker) gameplay.

<Note>
  This client was previously private for 5 years and has been used exclusively by a small group of top-tier players. It's designed around a specific playstyle and may require time to learn.
</Note>

Sn0w contains many original features crafted over years of development:

- **Fast Phasewalk** - Vertical and horizontal phase movement
- **Predict Surround** - Predictive surround placement
- **Doublemine Bypass** - Working doublemine for strict servers
